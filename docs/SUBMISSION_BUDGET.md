# Explicit path budget

Hard cap: 8,388,608 bytes of added submission files. Final path allocation: `dist/` at most 6,700,000 bytes; `src/` at most 410,000; `docs/` at most 115,000; `artifacts/` at most 845,000; all remaining source/tests/tools/manifests at most 318,608. These allocations sum to the hard cap. Exact measured totals, required-file presence and absence of packaging artifacts are recorded in `artifacts/submission-summary.json`; `node tools/check-budget.mjs` checks the complete tree.

The root .gitignore excludes dependency/cache directories at every nesting level, scratch evidence (removed by the runner), local secrets and logs. It does not exclude dist or deliverable source. No submodules, dependency archives, model weights or vendored npm registries are submitted. Five optimized derivatives of the fetched original GLBs remain complete local runtime assets. Original GLBs are reproducibly available from the pinned source archive; duplicate raw models are removed after optimization. Original WAVs remain for baseline integrity and audio fallback. AI runtime libraries are fetched at pinned public URLs on first chat, like the existing remote model weights; their licenses and provenance remain local.
